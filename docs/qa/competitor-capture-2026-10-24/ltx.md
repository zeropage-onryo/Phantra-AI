# LTX Studio — capture 2026-10-24

Signed in as Michael Massaad on app.ltx.studio, on the free plan ("100% Credits", "Upgrade").
**0 spent.**

**LTX has no chat agent to capture on this account.**
- Home offers Gen Space, Canvas, Storyboard, Video Editor and Flows (plus Dubbing and Caption).
- **Gen Space** is a direct generator: one box ("Use @Element to add characters, e.g.,
  '@Character walking in the park'") with chips Image · Nano Banana Pro · 1K · 16:9 · 3, and
  Generate. There is no conversation and no questions.
- **Storyboard Builder** is LTX's nearest thing to an agent: a script, idea or file in, a shot
  list out. The box reads "Start with your story idea, scene or full script — anything that
  sparks your vision…". It has chips ASPECT RATIO 16:9 · MODEL Nano Banana Pro · STYLE Add
  style, plus "Drag script file here" and "Blank Storyboard". Its button reads "**Upgrade to
  continue**", so it can't run on the free plan. We did not upgrade: that would be a purchase
  outside the brief.

What this shows about LTX's shape: frame, model and style are fixed on the board before any
writing. Characters are named elements, called with `@`, the same idea as our Elements.
Capturing the Storyboard Builder needs a paid LTX plan, which is Mike's call.
