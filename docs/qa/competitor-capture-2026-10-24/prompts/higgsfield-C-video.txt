Photorealistic slow still-life shot, locked-off camera on a tripod with only a very slow subtle push-in. The jar of honey sits on the oak table in warm morning sunlight: sticky honey still clings to the wooden dipper and a thick amber thread slowly stretches and falls into the jar, the last bead wobbling before it drops. Dust motes drift and turn in the shaft of sunlight, the sun glow through the glass honey shifts slightly as the light warms, and the linen cloth edge stirs faintly in a breath of air. Gentle depth-of-field bokeh on the dried wildflowers and toast in the background. Natural, unhurried, tactile food cinematography; no people, no hands, no text.
