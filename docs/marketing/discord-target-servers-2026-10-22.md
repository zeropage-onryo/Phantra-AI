**Draft, not sent. Mike to review.** 2026-10-22. Group 11 stretch of
`docs/tasks/task-overnight-2026-10-22.md`. The bot is `bots/discord/` in the
`zeropage-onryo/phantra-growth` repo since 2026-10-22 (`docs/tasks/task-bots.md` there): a public installable app, `/look` runs one
of five free, non-generative looks (Film Grade, Noir, 2000s Flash, Crash-Zoom Hook, Vertical

Nothing here has been sent, joined or installed.

## How to read the list

- **Verification.** `discovery` = Discord's own server-discovery page (name, member count and
  invite read from discord.com/servers/...); `site` = the invite was read from the tool's own
  website or help centre; `resolves` = the invite page answers with the server's name but the
  link came from an unofficial source; `hive` = the invite is on The Hive Index (a directory),
  not resolved; `dbl` = only a Discord Bot List join page (third-party redirect), **unverified**.
  Member counts are the listing's snapshot on the date given and move daily.
- **Fit** is audience match times the chance an admin adds a third-party bot. A tool vendor's
  own server (Kling, Higgsfield, Runway...) has the right audience and the lowest chance:
  most forbid other tools' bots outright. Community-run editing and AI-video servers rank
  higher for that reason even when smaller.
- **Before asking**, read `#rules` (or `#info`, `#welcome`) for the bot, self-promotion and
  advertising lines; the "rule to read" column names the one to find. None of these rules were
  readable from here: every server's channel list is behind the join.
- **Admin to approach** is a role. Discord shows the owner with a crown in the member list;
  `#rules` or `#staff` usually names the moderator role; for a vendor server, the community or
  developer-relations lead, found on the server's `#announcements` or the company's site.
  Never a personal name here.

## The admin pitch (under 150 words)

> Hi, I run Phantra (phantra.ai), a small AI production studio. We built a free Discord app
> for editors and creators: drop a clip (up to 15 s / 100 MB), type `/look`, pick Film Grade,
> Noir, 2000s Flash, Crash-Zoom Hook or Vertical Ready, and the bot posts the graded clip back
> in a thread. No AI generation, no credits, nothing to sign up for.
>
> It never DMs members (one optional welcome on join, which you can switch off), never posts
> unasked, only answers slash commands, and asks for the minimum permissions (send messages
> and files in threads). It costs your server nothing and we don't collect member data.
>
> Install link: `https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=<APPLICATION_ID>&scope=bot%20applications.commands&permissions=<PERMISSIONS_INTEGER>`
>
> Would you be open to adding it to one editing or showcase channel for a two-week trial,
> and removing it if it is ever noise?
