# Cost per effect and per model, 2026-10-22

Generated by `venv/bin/python -m ops.cost_table --write` (report-only, re-runnable). Every USD is the provider's price off the dated table in the code, and every credit figure is what a hold takes for it (`pricing.credits_for(pricing.usd_micros(usd))`, the pair `ledger.charge_credits` delegates to). Nothing here was run; no credit was spent.

- `pricing.MARKUP` = **2.4** (credits = provider USD x 100 x markup, rounded up)
- `pricing.CREDIT_FLOOR` = **10** credits per billable render, still or effect
- one credit = **1 cent** at retail on every plan (Standard $15/1500, Pro $35/3500, Max $95/9500); the top-up is $10/750 (1.33c)
- "implied markup" = credits x $0.01 / provider USD. Above 2.4x means the floor or the round-up set the price; **BELOW COST** would mean the credits are worth less than the provider's invoice at retail parity.

## Rows priced below cost

**None.** With `MARKUP` at 2.4 and the round-up in `credits_for`, every row charges at least 2.4x the provider's price; the only way a row goes below cost is a provider price that rose after its `checked` date without the table moving, which this script cannot see. Re-check the dated rows before a price-sensitive launch.

Rows where the 10-credit floor, not the markup, set the price (the implied markup is above 2.4x): `add-sound`, `flux2-pro`, `remove-background`, `seedream-edit`, `voice preview (eleven-v3) 50 chars`.

What the credits do NOT cover: the brain's model text (free on a plan, carried by the markup; the trial gets `pricing.trial_thinking_usd()` = $1.00 of Gemini text once), storage on R2, and Stripe's cut.

## Free-grant math: the Founding 500

The sprint doc's offer, month one, worst case: **500 members** x **300 signup credits**, share bonuses of up to **300 credits per member per month**, **4 weekly prizes of 2,500 credits**, against a **$3,100 retail ceiling** (one credit = one cent).

Two ways to read a credit given away:

- **Retail value**: credits x $0.01 -- what the same credits would have sold for.
- **Provider cost**: credits x $0.01 / 2.4 -- what fal or Gemini bills the studio when every one of them is spent (the hold is provider USD x 2.4 rounded up, so the real cost is at most this; the floor and the ceil() only lower it).

| scenario | signup credits | bonuses | prizes | total credits | retail value | provider cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| the doc's grant (300/member) | 150,000 | 150,000 | 10,000 | 310,000 | $3,100.00 | $1,291.67 |
| today's default `PHANTRA_SIGNUP_CREDITS` (100/member) | 50,000 | 150,000 | 10,000 | 210,000 | $2,100.00 | $875.00 |

Arithmetic, the doc's grant: 500 x 300 = 150,000; + 500 x 300 = 150,000; + 4 x 2,500 = 10,000; = 310,000 credits = $3,100.00 retail = $1,291.67 at the provider.

**The largest signup grant that keeps the worst case at the $3,100 ceiling:**

- ceiling counted at **retail** (the doc's own framing): (3,100 x 100 - 150,000 - 10,000) / 500 = **300 credits per member** -- exactly the doc's number; the offer is already sized to the ceiling, with no room above it.
- ceiling counted as **provider money** ($3,100 of real invoices at the real 2.4x markup): (3,100 x 2.4 x 100 - 150,000 - 10,000) / 500 = **1168 credits per member**.

Two things sit outside the credit arithmetic:

- the trial's included model text, $1.00 of Gemini per account once (`PHANTRA_TRIAL_THINKING_USD`): real money, up to $500 across 500 members;
- a `promo` lot expires after `ledger.EXPIRY_MONTHS` = 2 months (unspent credit costs nothing), so the worst case above assumes every credit is spent inside its window.

Neither the share bonus nor the weekly prize exists in code today: both would be hand grants (`ledger.grant(..., kind='promo')`). Grant them as `promo`, not `adjustment`: an `adjustment` lot counts as beyond the trial (`ledger.BEYOND_TRIAL_KINDS`) and lifts the once-only cap on the trial's model text.
