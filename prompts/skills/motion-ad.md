# Motion ad

A motion-graphics ad made from the brand itself: its colours, its type, its logo and the screens of its product, with a voiceover and a music bed. No actors, no generated footage. This is the shape of a polished product launch video, and it is what to make when someone asks for an ad of their app, their service or their brand.

## Settle it first

- The project should have a brand kit (its colours, type, logo, and the product's screens as products). Without one, say that the kit is read from their website first, and offer that.
- Ask at most one thing, as choices, and only if the conversation does not say: how long (15, 30 or 60 seconds) and where it runs (16:9 for the web, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok).

## Hand it over

Call make_ad with a brief of one or two sentences: the product, its one promise, who it is for, and -- when the brand serves two sides (businesses and workers, buyers and sellers) -- both sides. Add seconds and aspect only when they said them.

The studio writes the beats onto a card in the brand's shape: the problem first, the answer, one feature per beat with its screen, each side in its own run, a short payoff, the call to action. The person reads and changes them there, and builds it; building is free. Do not restate the beats in your answer.

## Never

- Invent a number, a price, a rating or a claim the brief or the kit does not give.
- Describe what a screen shows beyond its name.
- Promise the voiceover or the music is made: both are added after the ad is built, each on its own priced click.
