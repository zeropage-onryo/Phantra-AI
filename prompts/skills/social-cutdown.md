# Social cut-down

The same piece, re-shaped and shortened for where it will be posted, using what is already made. The free edits do this on the studio's own server: reformat for a new shape, trim_clip for a shorter version. Nothing is regenerated and no credits are spent.

## What each place wants

- TikTok, Reels, Shorts: 9:16, the strongest moment in the first second, usually under 15 seconds.
- Instagram or Facebook feed: 4:5 or 1:1.
- YouTube or a website: 16:9, the full length.
- A teaser or a bumper: 6 seconds or less.

## Settle it

Ask, as one question with choices, which places it is for -- skip it if they said. Then name the clip you will work from by its number. If there is more than one clip and they did not say which, ask.

## Pick the cut

For a shorter version, choose the range that holds the hook and the payoff, and say in one line why (for example: "2s to 9s: it opens on the pour and ends on the label"). The range must sit inside the clip.

## Pick the shape

- fit=pad keeps the whole picture with black bars. Safe, never loses anything, looks less native.
- fit=crop fills the frame and keeps the middle. Native-looking, but the edges are lost. Say what would be cut off, and use pad when the subject is not centred.
- When the frame needs to be EXTENDED rather than padded or cropped, that is the paid reframe effect (apply_effect). Offer it with that difference in one sentence; never choose it for them, because it costs credits.

## Make them

One edit per turn: a reformat or a trim_clip, on the number you named. Each lands as a new numbered result; the original is never touched. To do a trim AND a reshape, trim first, then reformat the trimmed result in the next turn. List what you will make before you start, and offer the next one when each is done.

## Check before you send

Is the source named by number? Is every range inside the clip? Did you say what a crop cuts off? Did you keep the free edits apart from the paid reframe?
