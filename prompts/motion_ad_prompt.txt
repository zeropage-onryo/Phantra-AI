You write the script of a short MOTION-GRAPHICS ad: no footage, no actors -- big
type, one huge faint word behind each line, product screenshots on cards, a small
label now and then, over flat brand colours. Think of a polished product launch
video: one idea per beat, a few words per idea, a clear arc from the problem to
the product to the call to action.

THE BRIEF FROM THE PERSON:
{brief}

THE BRAND:
{brand}

THE LENGTH: about {seconds} seconds in total, in {beats_min} to {beats_max} beats.
Most beats are 2-4 seconds; the opening and the call to action may be a little
longer. The beat seconds should add up to roughly {seconds}.

THE PRODUCT SCREENSHOTS the person attached, by number (a beat may show one of
them on a card; never invent a screenshot that is not listed):
{cards}

FOR EVERY BEAT write:
- "headline": the line on screen. Short -- at most 8 words, usually 3-6. Sentence
  case, ending with a full stop. Every headline different. No hashtags, no emoji.
- "big_word": ONE word, 3-10 letters, that sums the beat up (FIND, BOOK, RATE,
  SKILLS). It is set huge and faint behind the headline. "" for a beat that
  should not have one (the opening and the call to action usually do not).
- "card": the number of the screenshot this beat shows, or null. Use each
  screenshot where it fits the line best; a screenshot may appear more than once.
- "card_caption": a few words under the card ("Alex R. · Line cook"), or "".
- "label": a small pill of text -- a price, a time, a name, a place ("$24 / hr",
  "Sat 7:00 AM") -- or "". At most 4 words. Use it on a beat or two, not all.
- "background": which brand colour the beat sits on: "background" for most beats,
  "primary" for one or two beats that need to land hard (the product's name, the
  call to action).
- "seconds": how long the beat lasts.

Rules:
- Follow the brand's tone, its words to use and avoid, and its claims rules
  exactly. Make no claim about a product the brand does not allow.
- The last beat is the call to action, in the brand's call-to-action style when
  it has one.
- Write only the JSON object the schema asks for: a short "title" for the ad, and
  the "beats" in order.
